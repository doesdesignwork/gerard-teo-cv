Best choice for this trip: buy 2 EasyCards on arrival

At Taoyuan Airport MRT, buy one Adult EasyCard per person from a Card Vending / Add Value Machine or the station information counter. The standard empty card currently costs NT$100, then add stored value. Start with about NT$800–1,000 each and top up later if needed.

EasyCard · recommended

One card for Airport MRT, Taipei Metro, buses and many small purchases.

Card NT$100*
  • 1
    Where to buy: Taoyuan Metro and Taipei Metro station information counters; selected card vending machines; 7-Eleven, FamilyMart, Hi-Life and OK Mart.
  • 2
    Top up: Metro information counters, add-value machines and participating convenience stores. Taoyuan Metro machines accept top-ups in NT$100 multiples.
  • 3
    How to use: Touch the card at the gate when entering and leaving MRT systems. On buses, use the onboard EasyCard reader and follow the displayed tap instructions.

Taoyuan Airport MRT

For the airport ↔ A1 Taipei Main Station journeys.

EasyCard or single trip
  • A
    Easiest: use your EasyCard. Touch in at the airport station gate and touch out at A1 Taipei Main Station.
  • B
    Single-journey alternative: buy at the touch-screen ticket machine or station information counter. Machines support multiple languages.
  • C
    If buying a single ticket: select destination and quantity, pay at the machine, collect the ticket and change, then touch at entry and follow the exit-gate instructions.

Taipei Metro

For your city sightseeing days.

Single trips NT$20–65*
  • 1
    Recommended: continue using EasyCard. It removes the need to buy a ticket before every ride.
  • 2
    Single-journey tickets: available from Metro ticketing machines and all station information counters. QR single-journey tickets are also available through supported Taipei Metro channels.
  • 3
    Unlimited options: the current One-Day Pass is NT$150 and the 24-hour Pass is NT$180, both sold at station information counters. For this itinerary, pay-as-you-go EasyCard is the safer default unless you plan a very MRT-heavy day.
